How Study Place works

Three steps, repeated every day.

  1. Choose one goal

    Decide what you will finish today, such as one chapter or ten questions.

  2. Focus for 25 minutes

    Start the timer, silence your phone and work on that single goal.

  3. Rest, then review

    Take a 5-minute break. At the end of the day, recap what you learned.

Common questions

How long should I study each day?

Quality matters more than hours. Two to four focused hours with breaks usually beats a long, distracted day.

What if I cannot focus?

Start with just 10 minutes. Remove your phone from the room and begin with the easiest task to build momentum.

How do I remember what I read?

Close the book and explain the topic in your own words. Then check what you missed and repeat it a day later.

Is Study Place free?

Yes. It is a free website with no account and no ads.
